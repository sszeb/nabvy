This policy is part of the [Nabvy Terms of Service](terms.md). It explains the limits behind each plan, what counts as excessive or abusive use, and what we may do about it.

## 1. Why this policy exists

1.1 **Shared searches.** Nabvy runs one shared search for each area and product that people are hunting, and every hunt in that area uses it. Each search costs us money to run.

1.2 **Paid checks.** Checking features cost us money each time they are used. They include scans, lookups and similar-item searches, and, on the Business plan, exports. [OWNER TO CONFIRM: boosts, as in terms 4.4.]

1.3 **Fairness.** Plans and allowances are priced for normal use by one person. This policy keeps the service fair and working well for everyone, and protects the sellers whose listings we show.

## 2. What normal use looks like

2.1 **Free, Standard and Pro.** These plans are for one person using Nabvy for their own buying and reselling decisions. That means setting up hunts for items you want to find, reading your feed and alerts, checking listings that interest you, and scanning items in front of you.

2.2 **Business.** The Business plan is for a business's own use of Nabvy. That includes the exports, channel feeds and API access that come with the plan, within the limits below.

## 4. Excessive or abusive use

Use goes beyond normal use when, for example:

- 4.1 **Automated or bulk querying.** You use bots, scripts or scraping tools, or send rapid or repeated requests beyond what a person using the app would make. For Business, this means going beyond normal use of the API within its limits.
- 4.2 **Mirroring the market.** You create many hunts, areas or searches to copy or monitor the market as a whole, rather than to find items you want.
- 4.3 **Account sharing.** More than one person uses an account, or one paid plan serves a group.
- 4.4 **Reselling alerts.** You resell, forward or republish alerts, feeds or data, except for Business exports, channel feeds and API access used as that plan allows.
- 4.5 **Multiple accounts.** You open more than one account, for example to get more free usage, trials, bonus usage or referral credits.
- 4.6 **Unusual load.** Your use puts unusual load on Nabvy or our data providers, or is far above what other people on the same plan do.
- 4.7 **Scans at scale.** You scan in bulk, or scan images that are not items you are dealing with, for example to build a catalogue.
- 4.8 **Chargebacks.** You dispute payments that were properly taken for a service you received or used.

## 5. What we may do

5.1 **The steps.** If we notice excessive or abusive use, we may take one or more of these steps:

- **(a) Notice.** We send you a short notice in the app or by email under our terms.
- **(b) Throttling.** We slow down your requests, searches, refreshes or alerts, or limit how quickly you can run paid actions.
- **(c) Limits on hunts or alerts.** We cap or pause some of your hunts, stop you adding new ones, or limit how many alerts you receive.
- **(d) Feature restrictions.** We turn off particular features for your account, such as scan mode, top-ups, exports, channel feeds, API access, trials or referral rewards.
- **(e) Temporary suspension.** We suspend your account until a date we set, or until we lift the suspension (section 10 of the terms).
- **(f) Permanent ban.** We close your account permanently and do not allow a new one (section 10 of the terms).

5.2 **Choosing a step.** We choose the step by how serious the use is, whether it has happened before and the risk it creates. For ordinary over-use, we usually start with the lighter steps. Where use looks like abuse or fraud, or puts other people or the service at risk, we may go straight to any step, including a ban.

5.3 **Promotional usage.** We may also reverse bonus usage and referral credits gained through the excessive or abusive use.

## 6. These steps are automatic

6.1 **Automated systems.** Our automated systems watch usage and apply the steps in section 5 themselves. A person does not need to be involved, and a step can apply without a warning first.

6.2 **The notice.** When a step applies, you receive a short notice in the app or by email saying that your account has been restricted under our terms and how to ask us to review the decision. If a suspension is temporary, we tell you the date it ends.

6.3 **No reasons.** Except where the law requires us to, we do not give reasons, and we do not share the rules, evidence, signals, thresholds or scores behind any step, because that would help people get round them.

6.4 **Reviews.** If you think we have made a mistake, you can ask us to review the decision (section 10.4 of the terms).

## 7. First time and repeat over-use

7.1 **First time.** For use that does not look like abuse, we usually start with the lighter steps in section 5.1 (a) to (d), and wait [OWNER TO SET: grace period, for example 7 days] before we suspend your account.

7.2 **Repeat.** If it happens again within [OWNER TO SET: period, for example 3 months], we may suspend your account without starting with the lighter steps.

7.3 **Abuse.** Abuse, fraud and security risks do not get a warning or a grace period.

## 8. What you keep during a restriction

8.1 **Restrictions short of suspension.** While your account is restricted but not suspended (steps (a) to (d)), you can still:

- sign in;
- see your account and data, and download your data;
- use the billing portal, including to cancel;
- contact us.

8.2 **Suspensions and bans.** Sections 10.5 to 10.8 of the terms apply.

## 9. How restrictions are lifted

9.1 **Reaching an allowance.** Running out of an allowance is not a penalty. When your balance or an allowance runs out, the app offers a top-up or an upgrade. The limit lifts when your allowance resets, when you buy a top-up or when you upgrade.

9.2 **Throttling.** Throttling lifts when your use returns to normal.

9.3 **Other restrictions and suspensions.** These lift on the date we set, or when we lift them after a review.

9.4 **Bans.** A ban is permanent unless we lift it after a review.
