This policy is part of the [Nabvy Terms of Service](terms.md). It applies to everyone who uses Nabvy, including through the app, alerts, exports, channel feeds and the API.

## 1. The basic rule

Use Nabvy lawfully, honestly and with respect for other users, for sellers and for the service. Nabvy is for finding items and making your own buying and selling decisions. The rules below say what that means in practice. They apply to what you do yourself and to anything you help, allow or ask someone else to do.

## 2. Nabvy's service and data

You must not:

- 2.1 scrape, crawl or harvest Nabvy, or access it with bots, scripts or other automated tools. The only exception is the Business plan's API, used within its limits;
- 2.2 copy Nabvy's listings, alerts, price information, labels or other data in bulk;
- 2.3 resell, republish, share or redistribute Nabvy's feeds, alerts, exports or data, or use them to build a database, a competing product or a public feed. The only exceptions are the exports, channel feeds and API included in the Business plan, used as that plan allows (section 9.4 of the terms). TODO-LEGAL (LR-11)
- 2.4 copy, frame or mirror any part of Nabvy;
- 2.5 decompile or reverse engineer the app, except where the law allows it.

## 3. Sellers

Nabvy never shows who a seller is, and it never messages sellers for you. When you contact a seller yourself, you must not:

- 3.1 send spam, bulk or copy-and-paste messages to many sellers, or unwanted repeated messages;
- 3.2 harass, threaten, insult or pressure a seller, or keep contacting a seller who has said no;
- 3.3 mislead a seller about who you are or what you intend.

You must not try to identify sellers. In particular, you must not:

- 3.4 try to find out who a seller is, where they live or how else to contact them, using Nabvy or by combining Nabvy's data with other information;
- 3.5 link listings to a particular person, or build profiles of sellers;
- 3.6 track or monitor a particular seller;
- 3.7 use pickup planning for anything except collections you have arranged yourself.

## 4. Accounts

You must not:

- 4.1 open more than one account for yourself, unless we have agreed in writing;
- 4.2 open accounts with scripts or other automated means, in bulk, or with temporary or disposable email addresses;
- 4.3 give false details or pretend to be someone else;
- 4.4 share your account or sign-in with anyone, or lend, sell or rent your account. Each account is for one person;
- 4.5 let a person we have banned use your account, or open an account for them.

## 5. Limits, trials, credits and bans

You must not:

- 5.1 get round any limit, throttle, cap or restriction on your account. This includes rate limits, hunt and area limits, usage limits and the scan cap;
- 5.2 get round a suspension or ban, for example by opening a new account or using someone else's;
- 5.3 use VPNs, rotating proxies, several devices or several accounts to hide what you are doing or to get more than your plan allows; TODO-LEGAL (LR-33)
- 5.4 collect free trials, bonus usage or referral credits more than once, refer yourself, or misuse promotional codes.

## 6. Security

6.1 You must not probe, scan or test Nabvy for weaknesses, load-test it or try to bypass sign-in, captcha or any other security measure, unless we have given you written permission first.

6.2 You must not upload malware, attack or overload Nabvy, or do anything that places an unreasonable load on Nabvy, our data providers or other services we use.

6.3 You must not access, or try to access, another person's account or data.

6.4 If you find a security problem, please tell us at [SECURITY CONTACT] and do not use it or share it with anyone else.

## 7. Payments

You must not:

- 7.1 use a payment card or other payment method without the owner's permission;
- 7.2 test stolen or generated card details;
- 7.3 dispute a payment that was properly taken for a service you received or used (chargeback abuse).

## 8. Scan mode, AI and uploads

You must not:

- 8.1 upload photos or files you do not have the right to use, or anything unlawful, sexual, violent or hateful;
- 8.2 upload photos of other people, or of documents showing other people's personal details, where you can avoid it;
- 8.3 try to trick, jailbreak or manipulate Nabvy's AI features, or use them to produce harmful output.

## 9. Reports, feedback and records

You must not:

- 9.1 submit false reports, feedback, or "bought for" or "sold for" records. That includes records meant to change labels, price information or referral rewards;
- 9.2 misuse our report, complaint or appeal routes. That includes repeated reports made in bad faith, and opening accounts to ask about a restricted account.

## 10. The law and other people's rights

You must not:

- 10.1 use Nabvy for anything unlawful, including fraud or dealing in stolen goods;
- 10.2 infringe anyone's intellectual property, privacy or other rights.

## 12. What happens if you break this policy

12.1 **Steps we may take.** We may take any of the steps in the [Fair Use Policy](fair-use.md) and in section 10 of the terms, "Suspension and bans". They include:

- restricting or slowing down your account;
- limiting your hunts or alerts;
- suspending your account for a time;
- banning it permanently;
- refusing to let a banned person open a new account.

12.2 **Other actions.** We may also remove content and reverse promotional usage or referral credits gained by breaking this policy.

12.3 **Automatic enforcement.** Enforcement is automatic. You receive only a short notice, and, except where the law requires us to, we do not give reasons (section 10.3 of the terms). You can ask us to review a decision (section 10.4 of the terms).

12.4 **Monitoring.** We use automated systems to detect abuse. We do not promise to monitor everything that happens on Nabvy.

12.5 **Reporting to the authorities.** Where we believe something unlawful has happened, we may report it to the police, regulators or other authorities and share relevant information with them, as the [Privacy Notice]([PRIVACY NOTICE URL]) describes. TODO-LEGAL (LR-27)

12.6 **No refunds.** Paid amounts are not refunded when we act under this policy, except where required by law (section 10 of the [No-refunds and Cancellation Policy](refunds-and-cancellation.md)).
