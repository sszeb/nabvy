## 1. The short version

- **No refunds.** Payments are non-refundable, except where required by law.
- **Cancel at any time.** Your plan runs until the end of the period you have paid for, and you are not charged again.
- **Reminders.** We email you before a free trial ends and before an annual plan renews.
- **Failed actions.** If a paid action fails, its usage goes back to your balance.
- **Your legal rights.** Nothing in this policy affects your legal rights.

4.1 The 7-day Standard trial needs a payment card. We do not charge you if you cancel before the trial ends.

4.2 We will email you before the trial ends. The email gives the date the paid plan starts, its price and how to cancel.

4.4 Bonus usage that comes with the trial expires when the trial ends. It has no cash value.

4.5 Trials are limited to one per person.

## 6. How to cancel

6.1 **In the billing portal.** Open your account page and choose to manage your billing. The billing portal lets you cancel in a few steps.

6.2 **By email.** Write to [CONTACT EMAIL] from the email address on your account. We will cancel your plan and confirm by email.

6.3 **When it takes effect.** Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. Until then, you keep your plan, its features and its usage allowance. After that, your account moves to the Free plan. Your hunts and data are kept, but only the Free plan's features and allowances apply.

6.4 **During a trial.** If you cancel during a free trial, you keep the trial until it ends and are not charged.

6.5 **Cancelling is not deleting.** Cancelling your plan does not delete your account. Deleting your account is covered in section 7.5 of the terms. It ends your access once the deletion is complete, and any remaining paid time and usage balance are lost.

## 7. Changing your plan

7.1 **Upgrades.** A move to a more expensive plan takes effect straight away. We charge the difference for the rest of your current billing period at once, as Checkout shows.

7.2 **Downgrades.** A move to a cheaper plan takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You keep your current plan until then, and no credit or refund arises, except where required by law.

7.3 **Extra areas.** Before you add an extra area, Checkout shows what you will pay. Removing an extra area takes effect at the end of your current billing period, with no credit or refund, except where required by law.

## 8. Usage balance

8.1 **Top-ups.** Purchased usage is non-refundable, except where required by law. It does not expire while your account is open.

8.2 **Included usage.** Your plan's monthly usage allowance expires at the end of each month and does not roll over.

8.3 **Promotional usage.** Trial bonuses, referral credits and other free usage have no cash value. We may remove them if they were obtained in breach of the terms.

8.4 **Failed actions.** If a paid action fails, the usage it cost goes back to your balance automatically. This returns usage to your balance; it does not refund money. If you think an action failed and your usage did not come back, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

8.5 **Closing your account.** When your account closes, any remaining usage balance is lost and is not refunded, except where required by law.

## 11. Payment disputes and chargebacks

11.1 **Talk to us first.** If you think we have charged you wrongly, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] before you contact your bank. We will look into it.

11.2 **If you raise a dispute with your bank.**

- We record the dispute. We may give your bank or card issuer our records of the payment, such as your confirmation at Checkout and your use of the service.
- While the dispute is open, we may treat it as a request to cancel the subscription the payment relates to.
- If the dispute is decided in your favour, your bank returns the money. We do not refund it a second time.
- We may reverse referral credits and other promotional usage linked to a disputed payment.

11.3 **Chargeback abuse.** Chargeback abuse means disputing a payment that was properly taken for a service you received or used. It breaks the [Fair Use Policy](fair-use.md) and the [Acceptable Use Policy](acceptable-use.md). It may lead to a suspension or a ban under section 10 of the terms.
