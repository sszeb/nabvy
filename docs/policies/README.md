# Policies (drafts)

Drafts of Nabvy's user-facing policies, written on 2026-09-24:
- `terms.md`: Terms of Service;
- `refunds-and-cancellation.md`: No-refunds and Cancellation Policy;
- `acceptable-use.md`: Acceptable Use Policy;
- `fair-use.md`: Fair Use Policy.

How they were written:
- They follow the owner's decisions in `docs/decisions.md`: "No refunds", "Fair use, suspension and bans" and "Policies and conduct match big tech".
- They match the positions big tech takes in its UK-facing terms, and Apify's and Supabase's for platform-style clauses, in Nabvy's own words. `SOURCES.md` maps each section to what it follows.
- `[OWNER TO ...]` marks a choice for the owner. `[PLACEHOLDER]` marks a detail still to fill, such as the company name or contact addresses.
- `TODO-LEGAL (LR-nn)` marks a point listed in `docs/legal-review.md`. No legal review has been run (owner's instruction).

The web app publishes the approved versions as its legal pages (task 4.7).
