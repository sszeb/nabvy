## Principles carried from the decisions

1. Stripe is the source of truth for paid entitlements; no paid entitlement without a Stripe event (`docs/billing.md`, "Entitlements").
2. No prices in code: plan price IDs are `pricing-console` ladder rows; only the top-up packs and the extra area are env price IDs (PR #54's choice, kept).
3. No refunds, no refund path, cancel at period end, downgrades at period end, upgrades charged at once (`docs/decisions.md`, "No refunds").
4. Prices are in GBP and include UK VAT ("Pricing and cadence"; Stripe Tax).
5. Keys only through `@nabvy/config` from `docs/secrets.md`; never printed, never in code, commits, logs or chat.

## Billing

**Subscriptions on the paid ladder.** One Stripe Product per tier (Starter, Pro, Max, Business), each with a monthly and an annual recurring Price in GBP, `tax_behavior: inclusive`, and a `lookup_key` (`starter_monthly`, `starter_annual`, …) so a price can be re-created without editing rows. Extra area is one Product with a monthly Price sold by quantity. The ladder's numbers (base cadence, floor, bundled credits, price) are `pricing-console` policy rows ("Paid ladder": "never a constant in code"); the Stripe price ID lives on the row, and `subscriptions` reads it through `SubscriptionsLadderPolicy` (PR #54, `services/subscriptions/src/index.ts:65-82`). A ladder change that alters a price creates a new Stripe Price and a new policy version; existing subscribers stay on the old Price until moved (a Stripe Price is immutable).

**Bundled credits.** Not Stripe metered billing. Each paid invoice that took money (`billing_reason` `subscription_create` or `subscription_cycle`) grants the plan's bundle through `usage-ledger.grantAllowance` with refId `allowance:<sub>@<window start>`, expiring at the window's end; annual plans get a monthly window from the sweep `grantDueAllowances` (PR #54, `stripe.ts:366-434`, `index.ts:334-370`). Credits never appear in Stripe, so Stripe's invoice shows the fee only, which is what the tier sells. Keep this: Stripe usage-based billing would put credit prices on Stripe's side, against "prices are dynamic" and the always-profitable floor in `pricing-console`.

**Top-ups.** One Product "Usage top-up" with three one-off Prices (£5, £10, £25, tax-inclusive) bought through a payment-mode Checkout (`index.ts:219-258`); the `checkout.session.completed` webhook grants credit through `grantTopup` with the PaymentIntent as refId, only when `payment_status = paid`, and `async_payment_succeeded` grants later for delayed methods (`stripe.ts:441-495`). Bundle discounts per tier are `usage-ledger`'s `topupCredits` policy, so the Stripe Price is the cash and the policy is the credit.

**Proration.** Upgrades: `proration_behavior: always_invoice` so the difference is invoiced and charged at once (decision). Downgrades: a subscription schedule that switches at the period end with `proration_behavior: none` (the plugin does this when the upgrade call carries `scheduleAtPeriodEnd: true`). The invoice a mid-cycle upgrade raises has `billing_reason: subscription_update`; PR #54 records it and grants no bundle, and asks in `docs/questions/subscriptions.md` whether the new plan's bundle should start at once. Recommendation for the owner's answer: grant the difference in bundles pro rata at once, since the user paid the difference at once; until answered, PR #54's conservative choice stands.

**Dunning.** Stripe Smart Retries with the dashboard's default schedule; after the last retry the subscription is **cancelled** (dashboard setting "Cancel the subscription"), so `customer.subscription.deleted` drops the entitlement to Free. Do not choose "Mark as unpaid" or "Leave as past-due": PR #54's matrix keeps the plan on `past_due` while retries run (`domain/index.ts:29-38`), and a subscription left `past_due` forever would keep the plan forever. Failed-payment emails: Stripe's own (dashboard, "Send emails about failed payments") at launch; PostHog Workflows from `billing_events` later (`docs/marketing.md`). Renewals that need 3-D Secure raise `invoice.payment_action_required`; Stripe emails a hosted invoice link when "Send emails when a payment requires action" is on. The app shows a banner while `status = past_due` from `v_entitlements`.

**Trial.** Starter only, 7 days, card required, through the plugin's `freeTrial` (`plugin.ts:35`). Set `subscription_data.trial_settings.end_behavior.missing_payment_method: cancel` in `getCheckoutSessionParams` (Checkout accepts it; the plugin spreads the returned params into the session, keeping only `mode`, `customer`, URLs and line items for itself). Since API version 2026-08-26 Checkout can also refuse prepaid cards (`payment_method_options.card.restrictions.funding_types_blocked: ['prepaid']`), which is the cheapest guard against repeat trials on throwaway cards; offer it to `account-integrity` (4.10j). The £3 trial bonus (`taste` grant) is not granted anywhere yet (4.10j). The plugin allows one trial per `referenceId`; account-integrity's card, email and device check ends a repeat trial through `endTrialNow` (`stripe.ts:333-340`).

**Free tier.** No Stripe object. The card check before free windows two and three ("Free tier", never charged, one card per account) is a Setup-mode Checkout (`mode: setup`, `payment_method_types: ['card']`) whose `setup_intent.succeeded` webhook records the card fingerprint in `billing_events` for account-integrity. Not in PR #54; belongs to `account-integrity`'s brief with a `subscriptions` function `startCardCheck` (4.10j).

## Payments

**Checkout.** Hosted Stripe Checkout for every purchase (subscription, trial, top-up, card check): Nabvy never sees card numbers, SCA and 3-D Secure are Stripe's, and the "Start my plan now" tick is `consent_collection.terms_of_service: required` with the non-refundable wording in `custom_text` (`index.ts:135-148`). Stripe needs a terms of service URL under Settings, Public details, or Checkout refuses the session (human task). `success_url` carries `{CHECKOUT_SESSION_ID}` so the account page can show "activating" until the webhook lands (`docs/billing.md`, "Upgrade").

**Payment methods for UK customers.** Leave `payment_method_types` unset and manage methods in the dashboard (dynamic payment methods), with this launch set: cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay and Google Pay (need the domain `nabvy.com` registered under Payment method domains), and Link. Off at launch: Bacs Direct Debit (3-day settlement, mandates, and a `payment_status: unpaid` top-up path that PR #54 handles but that the account page would have to explain), PayPal (available for UK subscriptions but adds a second dispute channel), Klarna and Clearpay (instalments on a non-refundable digital subscription; a question for the owner, not a build item). Card fingerprints from `charge.succeeded` feed `v_billing_signals` for ban evasion.

**Customer Portal.** The plugin's `billingPortal` endpoint opens a Portal session. One Portal configuration, created by the setup script (4.10e), with: update payment method on; invoice history on; cancel on, mode "at period end" only, no proration credit; plan switching off in the Portal (switches go through Nabvy's procedures so the ladder, the gate and the schedule rule apply); tax ID management on; business name and address editable. The account page links "Manage billing" to it.

**Off-session payments.** Renewals charge the saved payment method; Checkout sets it as the subscription's default. For top-ups, no saved-card charge at launch ("auto top-up … later option, off by default"); when built, use a PaymentIntent with `off_session: true` and the customer's default method, and handle `authentication_required` by emailing a hosted link.

## Tax

**Stripe Tax** is enabled on every Checkout (`automatic_tax: { enabled: true }`, `index.ts:146`) and on subscriptions (Checkout in subscription mode carries it to the subscription). Prices are tax-inclusive (`tax_behavior: inclusive` on every Price; the setup script refuses a Price without it), so a £29 plan is £29 to the customer and the invoice shows the VAT inside it. Nabvy's own VAT registration status and origin address are set once in the dashboard (Tax settings; the registration for the UK is added there when Nabvy registers). Below the UK VAT threshold Stripe Tax calculates nothing for the UK until a registration is added; the prices stay the same either way, which is why tax-inclusive is the right behaviour here. This is a fact about how Stripe Tax works, not tax advice; `docs/legal-review.md` lists the point.

**Tax IDs on invoices.** `tax_id_collection: { enabled: true }` on subscription Checkout so a business customer can enter a VAT number, and the Portal lets them add or change it later. Stripe prints the customer's tax ID and Nabvy's on invoices; reverse charge is not relevant for UK-to-UK sales. Sales outside the UK are out of scope at launch (Checkout can be limited to GB with `billing_address_collection` and shipping-less country restrictions, *verify* the current parameter); a question for the owner if non-UK sign-ups are wanted.

**Net cash.** PR #54 funds bundles from `amount_paid` less `total_taxes` (`domain/index.ts:183-189`), so the credit a subscriber gets is valued on what Nabvy keeps, which is what the always-profitable floor needs. Keep this.

## Invoicing

**Automatic invoices.** Stripe issues and emails an invoice for every subscription payment. Dashboard settings: invoice numbering per customer prefix, Nabvy's legal name, address and VAT number in the footer once registered, "Email finalised invoices" on, hosted invoice page on. The account page links to the invoice list through the Portal rather than fetching invoices itself.

**Receipts for top-ups.** Payment-mode Checkout does not create an invoice by default. Set `invoice_creation: { enabled: true }` on the top-up Checkout so a business customer gets a VAT invoice for a top-up too (4.10i); until then Stripe's payment receipt email covers it.

**Credit notes on refunds.** The refund policy is "no refunds"; the only refund is one the law requires, issued by the owner in the Stripe dashboard as "Refund with credit note" so the invoice, the VAT record and the credit note agree. The app must then react to `charge.refunded`: record a `refund` signal in `billing_events`, reverse the top-up's remaining credit through a `usage-ledger` reversal if the refund was a top-up, and reverse the affiliate commission through Dub (4.10g). No app or admin path creates a refund; that stays as designed. `credit_note.created` is recorded for the audit trail only.

**Chargebacks.** `charge.dispute.created` is recorded as a signal (PR #54); add `charge.dispute.closed` (won or lost) and `charge.dispute.funds_withdrawn` so a lost dispute drops the entitlement or reverses the top-up like a refund, and the consent record is the evidence submitted (4.10g).

## Connect

Nabvy sells subscriptions and top-ups and pays nobody through Stripe today. The affiliate programme pays partners through Dub Partners, which runs its own Stripe Connect platform (Express accounts under Dub, `docs/affiliates.md`); Nabvy only calls Dub's track-sale endpoint from the billing handler. Nabvy never takes payment for a marketplace item, so there is no buyer-to-seller flow to route. Connect would only be needed if: (a) the owner moves affiliate payouts off Dub onto Nabvy's own Connect platform (Express accounts, transfers, 1099-style tax handling for the UK equivalent), which `docs/affiliates.md` already rules against for the launch; or (b) a paid extra seat or partner reseller model appears. Neither is decided, so this doc designs nothing for Connect; the question is in `docs/questions/stripe.md`. Do not enable Connect in the dashboard until then: an enabled platform changes the account's compliance obligations (one line in `docs/legal-review.md`).

## Webhooks and events

The endpoint is `/api/auth/stripe/webhook`, served by PR #54's `createStripeWebhookRoute` wrapped around the plugin's handler, which verifies the signature a second time. Verified in the installed plugin: an error thrown from `onEvent` becomes a 400, so Stripe retries a failed event; the plugin's own lifecycle callbacks swallow errors, which is why PR #54 does not use them. Create the endpoint with the SDK's pinned API version (fixtures are on `2026-08-26.dahlia`) and only these `enabled_events`, so `billing_events` does not fill with `ignored` rows: `customer.created`, `customer.updated`, `customer.subscription.created`, `customer.subscription.updated`, `customer.subscription.deleted`, `invoice.paid`, `invoice.payment_failed`, `invoice.payment_action_required`, `charge.succeeded`, `charge.failed`, `charge.refunded`, `charge.dispute.created`, `charge.dispute.closed`, `charge.dispute.funds_withdrawn`, `checkout.session.completed`, `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded`, `checkout.session.async_payment_failed`, `setup_intent.succeeded`, `credit_note.created`. The `stripe_object_id` index makes the dispute and refund lookups cheap. Retention: `billing_events` is append-only and kept after account deletion for the consent record (PR #54's question; `docs/legal-review.md`).

## Review of PR #54

Head `9bcdc7e`, whole diff read through the API. The reviewer's round-1 findings 1 to 4 and 7 are fixed on this head (`payment_status`, terminal deletion, late invoices, idempotent `endTrialNow`, README wording); 5, 6, 8 and 9 are logged as questions. Findings below are new, against this plan. R1 is a money finding; the rest are not blocking.

- **R1 (money): upgrades use `create_prorations`, so the difference can go uncharged.** `services/subscriptions/src/handlers/plugin.ts:31-43` builds the plugin's plans without `prorationBehavior`, and the installed plugin defaults `stripe.subscriptions.update` to `proration_behavior: "create_prorations"`. The proration then waits for the next cycle invoice; a user who upgrades on day one and cancels at period end gets the higher plan for the month and the proration item is discarded with the cancelled subscription. The decision is "upgrades take effect at once and charge the difference". Fix: add `prorationBehavior: 'always_invoice'` to every plan in `toStripePlans`, and a fixture asserting it. One line plus a test. This path is unreachable until the plugin is mounted, so it is a merge-with-follow-up call for the coordinator; the fix is small enough for #54.
- **R2 (Checkout breaks): the top-up Checkout passes an existing customer with automatic tax and no `customer_update`.** `index.ts:246-256` sends `customer` when a link exists and `automatic_tax.enabled`; Stripe's tax guide for Checkout (`/tax/checkout/page`, "Calculate tax for existing customers") requires `customer_update.address: 'auto'` when Checkout collects the billing address for an existing customer, and a customer with no saved address fails session creation without it (confirmed through the Stripe MCP docs search). The plugin sets it for its own Checkout at its `customer_update` line, which is why subscriptions are fine. Fix: spread `customer_update: { address: 'auto', name: 'auto' }` into the `customer` branch only (it is invalid with `customer_creation`). Extend the `checkout.test.ts` session assertion.
- **R3 (policy): downgrades apply at once with a proration credit.** `index.ts:176-202` returns `{ plan, annual, metadata }` for the plugin's upgrade endpoint, never `scheduleAtPeriodEnd`, so a Max to Pro change prorates immediately and leaves a credit balance, against "downgrades are scheduled for the period end, so no credit arises". Fix: compare the target row's rank with the current entitlement's (`SubscriptionsLadderPlan` needs a `rank` or the ladder's order) and set `scheduleAtPeriodEnd: true` when the target is lower; a domain function `isDowngrade(current, target)` with a fixture. The plugin's schedule path uses `proration_behavior: none`.
- **R4 (robustness): the allowance reads `invoice.lines.data[0]`.** `stripe.ts:389-392`. A cycle invoice can carry proration or extra-area lines first; a proration line's period makes `intervalMonthsOf` return null, and the event then fails as `processing` on every retry, so the renewal's bundle is never granted and ops is alerted on each retry. Fix: pick the line whose `price.id` matches the ladder's plan price (add `price: { id }` to the invoice line schema at `packages/contracts/src/modules/subscriptions.ts:211-213`), fall back to the longest period, and add a fixture with a proration line first. R1's `always_invoice` reduces but does not remove the case (extra areas).
- **R5 (ordering noise): `charge.succeeded` ignores the charge's metadata.** `stripe.ts:198-213` resolves the user by customer only; the top-up Checkout already copies `userId` into `payment_intent_data.metadata` (`index.ts:253`), which the charge inherits. Passing `charge.metadata?.userId` to `requireUser` resolves a charge that arrives before `checkout.session.completed` for a new customer instead of failing as `unknown_customer` and alerting.
- **R6 (money shown to the user): the top-up quote and grant use different bases.** `startTopup` values the pack at the gross price (`index.ts:237-238`, `packPounds * 100`), the webhook grants on the net of tax (`stripe.ts:483`). With a policy of one credit per penny, a £10 pack is quoted at 1,000 credits and grants 833 (the test at `webhook.test.ts`, "a paid top-up Checkout becomes credit once", asserts 833). Fix: quote on the same net basis (gross less the VAT rate the policy row carries) or let `pricing-console`'s `topupCredits` define credits per pack and use the pack, not the cash, as the key; and show the credit count before Checkout from the same function. Owner wording is not touched.
- **R7 (currency): nothing asserts GBP.** `invoicePaid` and `checkoutCompleted` store `currency` but value cash in pence regardless. Refuse (`processing`) any invoice or session whose currency is not `gbp`; one line each, one fixture.
- **R8 (config): `extraAreaPriceId` is required by `subscriptionsStripeFromEnv`** (`plugin.ts:99`) though the type allows null "when extra areas are not sold" (`stripe.ts:60-61`). Make `STRIPE_PRICE_EXTRA_AREA` optional in the env group, or drop the null from the type. Cosmetic.
- **R9 (docs): the README's dashboard prerequisites are scattered.** Collect in one list: terms of service URL, tax-inclusive prices, Smart Retries ending in cancellation, Stripe Tax origin address, Portal configuration, Apple Pay domain, webhook endpoint with the API version and event list. 4.10e turns the list into a script where the API allows it.

Checked and sound, no change asked: signature verification on the raw body with a 300 s tolerance; advisory lock plus unique index plus same-transaction insert for idempotency; the replay answer before forwarding; `last_event_at` ordering and terminal deletion; `past_due` keeping the plan; net-of-tax bundles; `payment_status` gating; RLS and the append-only trigger; no `process.env`; no refund path; no key-like strings beyond dummy test values.
